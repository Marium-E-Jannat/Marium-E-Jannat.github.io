About Research Teaching Activities Beyond

Outside of research and teaching, I am usually busy making something, taking photos of something, or occasionally reading and watching comedy films and stand-up. My hobbies rotate between photography, sewing, hand embroidery, crochet, macramé, drawing and painting, and whatever new craft has managed to convince me that I absolutely need another set of supplies.
The kitchen is another one of my creative spaces. I love cooking traditional Bangladeshi food, especially experimenting with different vegetables, making all sorts of bhorta, and preparing homemade pickles (achar). Apparently, I have a hard time leaving things unexperimented with - whether it is yarn, fabric, a camera, or a perfectly innocent vegetable.
I also own three pairs of binoculars, which probably tells you something about how I like to explore. I love travelling through the small towns, villages, quiet localities, and landscapes of Western Canada - but I am decidedly less enthusiastic about walking. My husband and I go car camping for at least a week every year, and while other people may hike for hours to reach the perfect viewpoint, I am quite happy finding a good place to sit, pulling out my binoculars, and enjoying the farthest scenery from there. Why hike all the way over when I can zoom in?

Photography is one of my favorite ways to capture the world around me. I am particularly drawn to nature, quiet landscapes, and the small, unexpected moments encountered in everyday life and while travelling. I share some of these photographs through Unscheduled Shutters.

Making & Handcrafts

Much of my free time is spent making things. Depending on what has caught my interest at the moment, I might be sewing, working on hand embroidery, crocheting, making macramé, or experimenting with a new craft. I especially enjoy projects that involve working patiently with my hands and gradually seeing something take shape.

Food & Cooking

Cooking is another creative outlet for me and a way of staying connected to Bangladeshi food and traditions. I particularly enjoy making traditional dishes, experimenting with different kinds of bhorta, and preparing homemade pickles (achar). I love the variety of flavors that can come from simple ingredients — and I rarely stop at making just one kind of bhorta.
