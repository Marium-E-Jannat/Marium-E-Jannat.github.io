About

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at the Irving K. Barber Faculty of Science, UBC Okanagan, supervised by Mohammad Khalad Hasan, and now work as a Research Assistant in the Okanagan Visualization & Interaction (OVI) Lab. I began as an MSc student and transitioned into the PhD program. Before that I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at Shahjalal University of Science & Technology (SUST), Bangladesh, where I was also a faculty member.

My research sits at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction and ubiquitous computing: designing and evaluating interaction techniques for mobile, wearable and AR/VR devices, and studying how user preferences shape them. Earlier, I spent more than five years working in AI, machine learning and natural language processing. I have also collaborated on interdisciplinary projects involving technology, behavior and accessibility.
