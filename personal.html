Beyond Research

Outside of research and teaching, I am usually busy making something, taking photos of something, or occasionally reading and watching comedy films and stand-up. My hobbies rotate between capturing moments, sewing, hand embroidery, crochet, macramé, and whatever new craft has managed to convince me that I absolutely need another set of supplies. The kitchen is another one of my creative spaces. I love cooking traditional Bangladeshi food, especially experimenting with different vegetables, making all sorts of bhorta, and preparing homemade pickles. Apparently, I have a hard time leaving things unexperimented with — whether it is yarn, fabric, a camera, or a perfectly innocent vegetable.

I also own three pairs of binoculars, which probably tells you something about how I like to explore. I love travelling through the small towns, villages, quiet localities, and landscapes of Western Canada — but I am decidedly less enthusiastic about walking. My husband and I go road tripping for at least a week every year, and while other people may hike for hours to reach the perfect viewpoint, I am quite happy finding a good place to sit, pulling out my binoculars, and enjoying the farthest scenery from there. Why hike all the way over when I can zoom in?

Through My Lens

Photography is one of my favorite ways to capture the world around me. I am very much an amateur photographer — I just happen to take a lot of photos. I am particularly drawn to nature, quiet landscapes, the small and unexpected moments encountered in everyday life, and ordinary scenes that make me stop for a second and look again. Almost none of my photographs are planned; they are simply things I happened to notice along the way, which is why I call my little collection Unscheduled Shutters.

Exploring

I love road trips and exploring the small towns, villages, backroads, lakes, mountains, and quiet corners of Western Canada. My preferred style of exploration, however, involves considerably less walking than the word “exploring” might suggest. My husband and I go car camping for at least a week every year, usually picking a region and wandering around from there. And yes, the binoculars come with us. They are particularly useful when the interesting mountain, wildlife, or viewpoint is conveniently visible from somewhere I can sit.

Making & Handcrafts

When I am at home, there is a good chance that I will be on some unfinished project involving fabric, thread, yarn, rope, paint, glue, or an unreasonable number of tiny craft supplies that is occupying a corner somewhere. Depending on what has caught my interest at the moment, I might be sewing something, working on hand embroidery, crocheting, making macramé, or experimenting with a new craft — sometimes successful, sometimes simply adding another skill and several more supplies to the collection. Once, for several months, I also went through a glass-painting phase. Some of those paintings have survived all these years and are still stored back home. There is something particularly satisfying about starting with a pile of materials, working patiently with my hands, gradually seeing something take shape and eventually being able to say, “I made that.”

Plants & Gardening

More recently, I have developed a little interest in gardening and growing plants. I am very much a beginner, so at this stage gardening mostly consists of getting excited about a new plant and then worrying about keeping it alive. My current concern is an asparagus fern sitting beside the window. Winter is coming, I have questions, and the fern has no idea how mentally invested I have become in its survival. We will see how this goes.

Food & Cooking

My experiments continue in the kitchen as well. I love traditional Bangladeshi cooking and, to me, it is a way of staying connected to Bangladeshi food and traditions. I particularly enjoy making traditional dishes, experimenting with different kinds of bhorta, and preparing homemade pickles (achar). One of my favorite things about bhorta is that an astonishing number of perfectly ordinary ingredients can apparently be mashed, seasoned, and transformed into something delicious — which naturally makes me want to try all of them. I also enjoy making and preserving different kinds of spicy achar. I love the variety of flavors that can come from simple ingredients.

Reading & Watching

When I am not making, cooking, photographing, or tending to plants, I occasionally manage to sit still for entertainment too. I enjoy reading and watching comedy films and stand-up comedy. After spending much of my working day thinking seriously about research problems, I appreciate things that require considerably less analysis — and preferably make me laugh.
