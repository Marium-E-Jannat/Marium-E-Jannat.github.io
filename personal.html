Beyond Research

Outside of research and teaching, I enjoy photography, making things by hand, and cooking. My creative interests include sewing, hand embroidery, crochet, macramé, and various crafts. I also love preparing traditional Bangladeshi food, particularly different kinds of bhorta and homemade pickles.

Whether I am behind a camera, working with fabric and yarn, or experimenting in the kitchen, I enjoy the process of creating something from scratch.

Photography

Photography is one of my favorite ways to capture the world around me. I am particularly drawn to nature, quiet landscapes, and the small, unexpected moments encountered in everyday life and while travelling. I share some of these photographs through Unscheduled Shutters.

Making & Handcrafts

Much of my free time is spent making things. Depending on what has caught my interest at the moment, I might be sewing, working on hand embroidery, crocheting, making macramé, or experimenting with a new craft. I especially enjoy projects that involve working patiently with my hands and gradually seeing something take shape.

Food & Cooking

Cooking is another creative outlet for me and a way of staying connected to Bangladeshi food and traditions. I particularly enjoy making traditional dishes, experimenting with different kinds of bhorta, and preparing homemade pickles (achar). I love the variety of flavors that can come from simple ingredients — and I rarely stop at making just one kind of bhorta.
